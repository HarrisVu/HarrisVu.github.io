About

Models, markets
and financial innovation.

I am a Research Fellow at the AUT Center for Financial Research, Auckland University of Technology. My research combines theoretical and empirical approaches in finance and economics, covering asset pricing, behavioural finance and corporate finance.

My research agenda includes fintech, applied artificial intelligence and machine learning in finance, quantitative finance, economic modelling and econometrics. Across these areas, I develop and test models to understand financial markets and economic decisions, with applications to investment, risk management and financial innovation.

I hold a PhD in Finance from the University of Auckland, where I studied media sentiment and emotions in commodity futures markets. Before working in New Zealand, I lectured at the National Economics University in Hanoi.
